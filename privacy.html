隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日 · 适用：今古方舆 iOS / iPadOS / macOS App

今古方舆由郭国提供。App 不要求注册账号，不包含广告或用于广告跟踪的 SDK。开发者不通过 App 收集个人笔记、轨迹、附件或持续定位数据，也不将这些内容上传至开发者服务器。

设备中的个人内容

你创建或导入的笔记、文档、照片、视频、录音、轨迹、个人巡览，以及收藏和阅读记录，在设备上保存和处理。个人笔记可以随系统设备备份保存，备份的存储位置和保护方式由你的系统设置及相应服务决定。iOS 的轨迹和个人巡览目录被排除在 iCloud 备份之外。Mac 资料保存在本机。App 不提供个人资料的自动跨设备同步。你主动导出或分享时，所选内容会交给你选择的目标 App 或服务，其后续处理受对方规则约束。

网页与电子书附件

网页与电子书附件在联网时会按文件本身的地址加载远程内容。文件中的链接、脚本或嵌入资源可能向相应网站发送请求，服务方可能接收到 IP 地址及常规访问请求信息，并按其自身规则处理。这些请求不经过开发者服务器中转。

权限和位置

App 在你使用相应功能时请求权限：相机用于拍照；照片权限用于选取媒体及读取拍摄时间、位置；麦克风用于录音；定位用于显示当前位置和记录轨迹。iPhone 和 iPad 在录制轨迹期间可使用后台定位，暂停或结束后停止。照片或视频自带的位置可用于地图显示。为查询位置名称，相应坐标会发送给 Apple 地图服务，返回的名称保存在本机。你可以在系统设置中关闭权限；对应功能可能因此无法使用。

第三方服务

Apple 底图和地理名称查询由 MapKit 等系统地图服务提供。历史资料和概览地形随安装包提供。三维地形细节及地图中心点海拔按需从 AWS 托管的 Mapzen Terrain Tiles 获取；服务方会接收到 IP 地址、瓦片编号及常规请求信息，瓦片编号对应浏览的地图区域。缓存保存在本机，App 不在瓦片请求中主动附加个人笔记、轨迹或设备定位坐标，开发者不通过自有服务器中转这些请求。Apple 地图和地名查询可能发送地图区域或查询坐标给 Apple 及其服务提供方；AWS 高程请求由对应服务方按其隐私规则处理。切换底图不代表停止所有网络请求。你打开史料或许可外部链接时，对应网站可能处理访问请求。App Store 的购买、支付和系统诊断由 Apple 按其服务规则处理，开发者不接收你的支付卡信息。可查阅 Apple 隐私政策。

试用与购买

App 使用 Apple StoreKit 处理七天试用与完整版本买断，并在设备上验证交易记录。试用的原始领取时间、购买状态等交易信息用于判断可使用的功能及恢复购买。试用到期不会自动扣费；购买由你主动确认。开发者不接收支付卡信息，不通过自有服务器保存购买记录。

AI 外部探索与文字复制

AI 功能通过与参考资料相同的系统 Safari 阅读窗口打开外部网站，关闭后回到原页面。App 不内置模型，不连接模型 API，也不读取网页正文或会话。你主动选择复制后，所选文字或当前条目摘要及已有出处会写入系统剪贴板；个人笔记不会默认加入条目摘要。打开网站时，App 不会将正文、笔记、轨迹或定位自动附加到网址或发送给 AI 服务。你自行粘贴和发送后的内容按所选网站的规则处理，系统剪贴板共享由你的系统设置控制。

首次使用时，App 可读取当前 App Store 商店地区来设置默认服务，不建立或保存地域档案。你选择的服务和自定义网址保存在本机，更新 App 后继续保留。为返回后继续阅读，App 在本机暂存地图视角、条目、阅读位置及当时编辑的笔记文字，关闭阅读窗口后清除这份暂存。各服务是否可用取决于服务商的地区、账号和服务规则。

保留、删除与选择

你可以在 App 内删除个人笔记和轨迹，或使用存储管理功能清理相应资料。删除 App 会移除其本地资料，系统备份、已导出文件和已分享的副本须在对应位置另行管理。开发者不持有可用于远程恢复的副本。清理或更换设备前，请自行导出并确认重要资料已保存。

支持邮件

你主动发送支持邮件时，开发者会收到邮箱地址和你选择提供的内容，并仅为回复、处理问题及必要的后续沟通使用。不用于广告或出售给第三方。可以通过下列邮箱提出删除支持邮件或其他隐私请求；依法需要保留的信息除外。

本网站

本网站不设置登录、分析统计或广告跟踪代码。网站托管服务商和邮件服务商可能为提供及保护服务处理必要的访问记录或通信数据。

联系与政策变更

隐私问题请联系郭国：ggsendy@gmail.com。App 的数据处理方式变更时，本页会更新适用说明与生效日期。

Privacy policy in English

Effective September 27, 2026. This policy covers the iOS, iPadOS and macOS apps. Jinggu Fangyu is provided by Guo Guo. No account is required. The app has no ads or advertising tracking SDKs. It does not upload personal notes, tracks, attachments or ongoing location records to developer servers.

Your notes, imported documents, media, tracks, personal tours, bookmarks and reading history are stored and processed on your device. Notes may be included in system device backups under your system and service settings. On iOS, the tracks and personal tours directories are excluded from iCloud backup. Mac data is stored locally. The app does not automatically sync personal data across devices. Files you choose to export or share are handled by the destination app or service.

When online, web and ebook attachments may load remote content from addresses contained in the files. Links, scripts or embedded resources in a file may send requests to the relevant websites. Those providers may receive your IP address and normal request information and handle them under their own policies. These requests are not relayed through developer servers.

Camera, photo library, microphone and location permissions are requested for the related functions. Location supports showing your position and recording tracks, including background recording on iPhone and iPad while a track is active. Recording pauses or ends when you choose those actions. Media may contain capture dates and locations. Coordinates are sent to Apple Maps to look up place names, which are then stored locally. You can revoke permissions in system settings.

Apple MapKit and system map services provide Apple basemaps and place-name lookups. Historical materials and overview terrain are included with the app. Detailed terrain and map-center elevation use Mapzen Terrain Tiles hosted on AWS. The provider receives the IP address, tile identifiers and normal request information; tile identifiers correspond to the viewed map area. Cached data stays on the device. The app does not add personal notes, tracks or device location coordinates to tile requests, and the developer does not relay them through developer servers. Apple map and place-name requests may include map regions or lookup coordinates. Each provider handles requests under its own privacy rules. Switching basemaps does not stop all network requests. External source links lead to websites with their own policies. Apple handles App Store purchases, payments and system diagnostics; the developer does not receive payment card details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

The app uses Apple StoreKit for the seven-day trial and one-time full unlock and verifies transactions on your device. Transaction information, including the original trial claim date and purchase status, is used to determine access and restore purchases. The trial does not automatically charge you when it ends. Purchases require your action. The developer does not receive payment card details or store purchase records on developer servers.

AI exploration opens external websites in the same system Safari reading window used for source references. Closing the window returns to the previous page. The app does not include a model, connect to model APIs, or read webpage content or conversations. Only when you choose to copy does the app place selected text, or the current entry summary and existing source references, on the system clipboard. Personal notes are not included in entry summaries by default. Opening a website does not automatically send or add text, notes, tracks, or location to its URL. Content you paste and send is handled under that website’s rules. System clipboard sharing follows your system settings.

On first use, the current App Store storefront may be used to choose an initial service; no regional profile is created or stored. Your service choice and custom URL are saved locally and retained across app updates. To resume reading after returning, the app temporarily saves the map view, current entry, reading position, and text of a note being edited on the device, and clears that temporary record when the reading window closes. Service availability depends on each provider’s regional, account, and service rules.

You can delete notes and tracks or use the app's storage controls. Deleting the app removes local app data; manage backups, exported files and shared copies separately. The developer cannot restore your data from a server. Export important information before removing the app or changing devices.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send, and uses it to respond and resolve the issue, not for advertising or sale. Contact ggsendy@gmail.com to request deletion of support correspondence or ask privacy questions, subject to applicable retention obligations. This website has no login, analytics or advertising tracking code. Hosting and email providers may process necessary service and security records. This page will be updated if the app's data practices change.
